Eras

From hand-checks to the crackdown: the cleanest and the most whistle-reliant scorers of each era

Each era is judged on its own terms: every component is standardized within its season, so a hand-check-era post scorer is compared with hand-check-era peers. A player’s era rating is the minutes-weighted average over at least two qualifying seasons in that era.

Era Seasons What changed
Hand-Check Era 1996-97 to 2003-04 Perimeter defenders could hand-check; slow, post-heavy offense
Freedom-of-Movement Era 2004-05 to 2012-13 Hand-checking banned in 2004-05; driving lanes open
Pace-and-Space Era 2013-14 to 2020-21 Three-point boom and peak foul-drawing craft
Post-Crackdown Era 2021-22 to 2025-26 Officials told to stop rewarding “non-basketball moves”

How the whistle changed

  • Three-point foul-drawing exploded and then got policed. Three-point shooting fouls rose from 0.77 per 100 attempts in 1996-97 to a peak of 1.82 in 2016-17, the peak of the leg-kick, lean-into-the-closeout era. They fell to 1.08 in the first crackdown season.
  • Shooting-foul free throws per shot went from 0.225 (1996-97) to 0.196 (2020-21) and 0.212 (2025-26).
  • Self-created offense has receded. The unassisted share of made field goals peaked at 43.6% (2009-10) and stands at 36.3% today, as offenses move the ball more.
  • Travels and offensive fouls have fallen from 2.92 to 1.76 per 100 scoring attempts.

Top 10 and bottom 10 by era

Each blurb is generated from the data: it names the two components that pushed that player’s rating up (top) or down (bottom) the most.

Show code 
era_gt <- function(e, which) {
  d <- tb[era == e & end == which, .(`#` = rank, Player = player, Seasons = seasons, EHI = ehi, Archetype = archetype, `Why` = blurb)]
  gt(d) |>
    fmt_number(EHI, decimals = 1) |>
    data_color(columns = EHI, fn = scales::col_numeric(c("#f2a6a5", "#f0efec", "#86b6ef"), domain = c(0, 100))) |>
    cols_width(`Why` ~ gt::pct(45)) |>
    tab_options(table.width = gt::pct(100), table.font.size = px(14), data_row.padding = px(4))
}

mix <- er[, .N, .(era, archetype)][, share := N / sum(N), era]
dcast(mix, era ~ archetype, value.var = "share")[match(eras, era)] |>
  gt(rowname_col = "era") |> fmt_percent(-era, decimals = 0) |>
  tab_header(title = "Archetype mix by era", subtitle = "Share of players with 2+ qualifying seasons in the era") |>
  tab_options(table.width = gt::pct(100))
