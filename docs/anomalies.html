Who loses the whistle in the playoffs?

The cliché says referees “swallow their whistles” in the postseason. For every player with 3+ qualifying-season playoff runs (and 300+ playoff field-goal attempts), we compare shooting-foul free throws per shot in the playoffs with the same seasons’ regular seasons, net of the league-wide change that year.

Show code 
pw_tbl <- function(d) d[, .(Player = player, `Playoff runs` = po_runs, `Playoff FGA` = po_fga,
                            `RS FTA/FGA` = rs_ftr, `PO FTA/FGA` = po_ftr, `Net change` = net_change, `% change` = pct_change / 100)] |>
  small_gt() |> fmt_number(c(`RS FTA/FGA`, `PO FTA/FGA`), decimals = 3) |>
  fmt_number(`Net change`, decimals = 3, force_sign = TRUE) |> fmt_percent(`% change`, decimals = 0, force_sign = TRUE) |>
  fmt_number(`Playoff FGA`, decimals = 0)
  • Biggest drops
  • Biggest gains

Spencer Dinwiddie shows the largest drop in Whistle Score (-1.20 SD). Statistical outliers

Any qualifying season where a component sits more than 3 SD from its season mean (component z-scores are capped at ±4 in the index). Each was checked for data bugs before being kept.

Show code 
ol[, .N, component][, .(Component = COMP_LABELS[component], `Seasons with |z| > 3` = N)][order(-`Seasons with |z| > 3`)] |> small_gt()
Component Seasons with |z| > 3
3PT shooting-foul rate 82
Perimeter and-one rate 63
Clean footwork (travels + offensive fouls, reversed) 46
Shot difficulty 26
Excess free-throw rate 21
Free-throw share of points 14
Shot-making above expected 10
Show code 
reactable(ol[, .(Season = season, Player = player, Component = COMP_LABELS[component], z, `Raw value` = raw,
                 `After shrinkage` = shrunk, FGA = fga)],
          searchable = TRUE, defaultPageSize = 10, theme = rt_theme, compact = TRUE, striped = TRUE)

Checked, and explained:

  • An early version of the shrinkage produced |z| > 8 for a handful of seasons. Tracing it showed that tiny samples (e.g. a big man with a couple of three-shot fouls on a handful of three-point attempts) were inflating the estimated spread of true talent. The priors are now fit only on players with reliable samples. The remaining outliers are large-sample seasons, genuine extremes rather than noise.
  • Very low shot difficulty belongs to lob-and-dunk centers whose attempts are almost all at the rim. That is a real shot diet, not an error.
  • High three-point foul rates belong to famously effective three-point foul-drawers.

Team effects (exploratory)

Do some franchises make players more or less “ethical”? A regression of EHI on player fixed effects + franchise fixed effects asks how a player’s EHI shifts when on one franchise versus elsewhere in their career. Relocated franchises are merged (e.g. SEA → OKC).

Show code 
te_tbl <- function(d) d[, .(Franchise = franchise, `EHI shift vs. own career` = effect, `Player-seasons` = player_seasons)] |>
  small_gt() |> fmt_number(2, decimals = 1, force_sign = TRUE)
  • Most “ethical” environments
  • Most whistle-friendly environments
